# The Shorthop name and logo

The code is free software under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE): you may run it, change it and share
it under that licence. The **name "Shorthop" and the Shorthop logo** are not covered by the
licence. This page says how you may use them.

## Fine without asking

- Running Shorthop, unmodified or changed, for yourself or your organisation, and calling it
  Shorthop inside that organisation.
- Redistributing the **official, unmodified** releases (image, chart) under their own name.
- Saying truthfully that something works with, connects to or is based on Shorthop — "an
  integration for Shorthop", "based on Shorthop" — in plain text, without the logo.
- Writing about Shorthop: articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons.

## Not without written permission

- **Forks** published or distributed under the name Shorthop or with the Shorthop logo. A fork
  is welcome — give it a different name and icon, especially in app stores.
- A **hosted service** offered to others under the Shorthop name, or one that suggests it is
  run or endorsed by Shorthop.
- Using the name or logo in your own product name, company name, domain name or app store
  listing.
- Changing the logo, or making something confusingly similar to it.

Ask at **support@shorthop.io** if you are unsure; the answer is usually yes.
